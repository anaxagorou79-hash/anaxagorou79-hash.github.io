HERMES DATAMIND / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-11-12. This policy covers the private Hermes Datamind Drive/Calendar desktop integration operated by Marios Anaxagorou.

Google information accessed

OAuth requests read-only access to Google Drive files and accessible Google Calendars. Initial connection checks use the account email, storage-quota metadata, and limited calendar-list metadata; they do not require file bodies or calendar-event contents.

For subsequent tasks, access is limited to information relevant to the owner's request. Drive content access requires the owner to select the folders first. A broader technical permission is not authorization to scan the whole account.

Use and AI processing

Relevant Google information may be processed by the cloud AI provider selected by the owner to perform the requested task, only with the owner's authorization. Such processing remains subject to the provider's applicable terms and data controls. The integration must not be used to send Google API data for training generalized AI models, advertising, data brokerage, or unrelated profiling.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Provider settings and handling must meet those requirements before Google content is transferred.

Storage and retention

OAuth client credentials and tokens are stored locally using Windows current-user DPAPI encryption. They are not hosted on this documentation website. Temporary task outputs and any owner-approved notes remain subject to the owner's retention choices. Google source data remains in the Google account; this integration is not a backup service.

AI providers and website hosts apply their own applicable retention and operational logging policies. This policy does not promise zero provider logging or unlimited token validity.

Sharing and control

Google information is not sold. Transfers are limited to the services needed for an authorized task or as required by law. Human access is limited to the owner and parties specifically authorized by the owner or permitted under Google's Limited Use rules.

The owner can revoke the app through Google Account connections, remove local encrypted credentials, and request deletion of locally retained task artifacts. Revocation does not delete the original files or events from Google.

This website

These static pages use no application analytics, cookies, forms, Google sign-in, or third-party scripts. The hosting provider may process standard request information under its own privacy policy. Public pages contain documentation and the operator's contact address, not private Google account data.

Questions and changes

Contact datamindanalytics2026@gmail.com for privacy questions or deletion requests. This policy must be updated if the integration's actual data handling changes.
